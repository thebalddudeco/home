GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Which offer should I choose?

Choose the one-off production for a brand event, activation, campaign, or product project. Choose the bi-annual partnership if your brand needs two planned image refreshes each year.

What happens before the shoot?

We’ll discuss your goals, audience, intended image use, people, location, timing, and shot priorities, then confirm the project scope.

How many final images are included?

The final image count is agreed in the written scope for your project, based on the production needs.

When will I receive the photographs?

The delivery timing is confirmed before booking and depends on the agreed production scope.

Can I use the photographs on my website and social channels?

Image usage and licensing are confirmed in writing for the intended channels before the project is booked.

What if I’m still figuring out what I need?

That’s fine. Start with what isn’t working or what’s coming up, and we can shape the shoot plan together.
