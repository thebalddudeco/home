Fragrance / L’Imperatrice / product story

Dolce & Gabbana

A fragrance study for Dolce & Gabbana L’Imperatrice, built around the bottle’s recognizable silhouette and the warm character of the set. The project pairs a product film with still photographs, using amber light and sculptural natural forms to give the glass and label a tactile setting.

The bottle

Keep the fragrance recognizable within a rich set.

The L’Imperatrice bottle remains the visual anchor throughout the series. Its glass, cap, and label are framed against warm tones and organic shapes, so atmosphere supports the product instead of obscuring its identity.

Sculptural light

Use the set to create depth around glass.

Warm illumination catches the bottle edges and lets the background fall away in layers. The camera moves between a full product view and tighter details, giving the campaign a mix of clear identification and material texture.

Supporting motion

Light moving across the bottle.

Film and stills

One product language across two formats.

The finished case pairs the lead film with three selected photographs and short motion studies. Still images hold the composition for close viewing; the moving pieces extend the same light, shape, and surface relationships over time.
