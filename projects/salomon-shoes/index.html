Footwear / Alphacross 5 / product story

Salomon

A product film and still series for the Salomon Alphacross 5, photographed in a dark, elemental setting. Rock and small stones ground the shoe in a tactile environment, while focused light separates its black silhouette and brings attention to the construction.

The Alphacross 5

Show the whole shoe and the parts that define it.

The project keeps the shoe’s shape as the first read, then moves closer to the upper, tread, and construction details. This progression helps viewers understand both the overall silhouette and the surfaces that make up the product.

Form against a dark field

Use contrast to reveal black-on-black detail.

A dark background preserves the minimal mood, but careful highlights trace the shoe’s edges and material changes. Rock and scattered stones add texture and scale without distracting from the Alphacross 5 itself.

Film followed by still detail

A product story built for both movement and inspection.

The cinematic film establishes the shoe in its environment; eight edited photographs hold on complete views and closer construction details. Together the formats offer an immediate impression of form and enough still detail for a slower look.
