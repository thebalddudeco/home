Brand project / case study

Salomon / Alphacross 5

A product film and still sequence for the Alphacross 5, shaped around silhouette, tread, and material.

Project focus
Footwear
Deliverables
Product film + stills
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

Project focus

What the story needed to show.

The selected set keeps the black Alphacross 5 against a dark field, using rock and small stones to give the shoe a grounded product context.

Creative approach

Keep the product and its point of view clear.

The still sequence moves from full-shoe views to close details of the upper, construction, and outsole. The light separates the silhouette from the background without losing the black-on-black character.

Final set

A finished story across motion and stills.

The finished cinematic film is followed by eight edited product photographs, including complete views and construction details.
