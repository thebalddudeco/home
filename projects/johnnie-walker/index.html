Brand project / case study

Johnnie Walker

A cinematic short built around a winter journey and the resolve to keep moving.

Project focus
Spirits / brand film
Deliverables
Brand film
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

Project focus

What the story needed to show.

The film’s narration uses cold, weather, and a long walk as its framing device, building toward the brand’s Keep Walking sign-off.

Creative approach

Keep the product and its point of view clear.

A measured voiceover carries the story through the winter setting. The pacing lets the landscape and the central figure hold the frame, keeping the focus on the journey.

Final set

A finished story across motion and stills.

The selected 44-second cut brings the voiceover and brand line together as one complete short film.
