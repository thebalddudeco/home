Electric commercial vehicles / brand film

Hino Max

A Hino Max brand film that approaches electric commercial vehicles through a driver’s point of view. Its narration connects work, health, and the future of driving to the changes underway in commercial transport; the accompanying photographs broaden the story with people, vehicles, and moments from the project archive.

Project focus
Commercial vehicles
Deliverables
Brand film
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

A driver’s point of view

Let the person give the technology its stakes.

The film starts with a working life rather than a specification sheet. A first-person narration links the vehicle to the practical realities of a driver’s career and the questions raised by a changing industry, giving the electric-vehicle subject a human point of entry.

People, work, and the vehicle

Keep the machine connected to its working context.

The edit carries the narrative through the vehicle and the people around it, so the truck reads as part of a real working environment. The accompanying archive photographs add further views of the team, the vehicle, and the project’s surrounding activity.

The finished piece

A 41-second film with a clear invitation forward.

The final cut moves from personal reflection toward the possibility of electric work, then closes with a direct invitation to consider what comes next. The ten additional photographs make the project page a fuller record of the people and vehicles connected to the story.
