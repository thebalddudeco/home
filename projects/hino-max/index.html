Brand project / case study

HINO MAX / Electric Legacy

A short brand film about commercial driving, personal stakes, and an electric future.

Project focus
Commercial vehicles
Deliverables
Brand film
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

Project focus

What the story needed to show.

The film frames an electric-vehicle story through a driver’s first-person perspective. Its narration moves from career, health, and future to the shift already underway in electric work.

Creative approach

Keep the product and its point of view clear.

The edit carries that point of view through the vehicle and the people around it, keeping the driver’s perspective at the center of the story.

Final set

A finished story across motion and stills.

The finished piece is a 41-second film with a clear narrative arc and a direct invitation to consider what comes next.
