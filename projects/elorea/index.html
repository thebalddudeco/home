Fragrance / natural materials / motion

ELOREA

A fragrance story for ELOREA shaped by contrasts between a precise glass bottle and natural, changing materials. Water, stone, moss, and smoke give the product a setting that feels tactile and atmospheric while leaving the packaging visible as the central form.

Project focus
Fragrance
Deliverables
Product film + stills
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

Natural materials

Place a precise object in an elemental world.

The selected photographs bring the bottle into contact with surfaces and movement drawn from the natural environment. Moss and stone provide texture; flowing water and smoke introduce softness and change around the hard edges of the glass.

A shifting palette

Move between freshness and shadow.

Cool greens and water suggest a clear, fresh register, while darker frames and warm light add weight and intimacy. The images vary in atmosphere but preserve a readable view of the bottle, making the shifts feel like parts of one fragrance identity.

Supporting motion

Water, smoke, and light in motion.

Across motion and stills

Let the materials continue to move.

The lead film establishes the world, the still sequence gives its textures time to register, and the short motion studies return to water, smoke, and light in concentrated passages. The formats work together as a flexible set of brand imagery rather than repeating the same frame.
