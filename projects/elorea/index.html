Project focus

What the story needed to show.

The selected photographs place the ELOREA bottle in natural textures: moss, flowing water, stone, and the movement of smoke.

Creative approach

Keep the product and its point of view clear.

The sequence shifts between cool greens and dark, warm tones while keeping the bottle legible and centered. The motion studies extend those material contrasts in short, focused passages.

Short product studies.

A finished story across motion and stills.

The finished set combines the lead product film, a selected still sequence, and the short motion studies supplied with the project.
