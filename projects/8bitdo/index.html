Hardware / product film / stills

8BitDo

A product film and still series that treats 8BitDo’s translucent keyboard and companion controls as both functional hardware and designed objects. The story uses a warm tabletop setting to make the colored shells, keycaps, switches, and control surfaces easy to read.

Project focus
Product photography
Deliverables
Brand film + stills
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

The objects

Make the design legible at a glance.

The set brings the translucent green keyboard and game controller into the same frame, then gives each object room for closer inspection. Their relationship matters: one is a full typing surface, the other a hand-held interface, and both share the same playful, transparent material language.

Light and detail

Let color and construction carry the story.

Warm wood gives the products a grounded surface while colored light moves across the transparent cases. Wider compositions establish the pair; closer views call attention to keycaps, buttons, and the small mechanical details people interact with.

The finished sequence

From complete setup to the details under your fingers.

The film supplies movement and progression, while the still sequence holds on the product long enough to examine its layout and finish. Together they present a complete product view and a set of useful close readings without separating the hardware from its visual personality.
