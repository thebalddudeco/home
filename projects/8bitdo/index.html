Brand project / case study

8BitDo

A tactile product film and still-image study built around color, controls, and the object itself.

Project focus
Product photography
Deliverables
Brand film + stills
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

Project focus

What the story needed to show.

The selected set centers 8BitDo hardware: a translucent green keyboard, companion controls, and close views of the materials and interface.

Creative approach

Keep the product and its point of view clear.

Warm wood, saturated light, and tight detail frames give the product a clear visual presence. The sequence moves from a complete tabletop view into the controls, keycaps, and small design details.

Final set

A finished story across motion and stills.

The finished set pairs the 8BitDo film with a consistent run of product stills, giving the project both motion and close-reading detail.
