NYC courthouse wedding / direct flash

Joyce + Aleks
in New York.

A New York courthouse wedding told from the ceremony through the city streets afterward. Joyce and Aleks’ photographs make room for the formal family portraits and the quick, unscripted moments between them, keeping the movement of the day part of the story.

A day that keeps moving

Carry the story beyond the courthouse doors.

The sequence begins with the wedding itself and follows the couple into the streets afterward. That change of setting gives the gallery a natural progression: ceremony, portraits, family, and the city becoming part of the celebration.

Portraits and in-between moments

Keep the people and their reactions close.

Direct-flash portraits bring the couple and their family forward with clarity, while candid frames preserve the transitions and exchanges around them. The gallery balances the images people expect from a wedding with the small moments that make this particular day feel lived in.
