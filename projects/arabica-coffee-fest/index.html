Arabica
Coffee Fest.

A documentary record of Arabica Coffee Fest told through the people moving through the space and the coffee that brought them together. The photographs follow the activation as a lived experience: visitors arriving, looking, talking, tasting, and returning to the details that caught their attention.

Inside the activation

Show how people met the experience.

The event story moves between the brand environment and the visitors inside it. Portraits and candid exchanges give the gathering a human scale, while the product and the surrounding setup explain what drew people into the space.

People and atmosphere

Keep the energy of the room in the frame.

The gallery builds from individual moments into a broader sense of the event: conversations, movement, coffee service, and the visual texture of the venue. The documentary film adds duration and soundless visual rhythm, carrying those encounters beyond a single still image.
