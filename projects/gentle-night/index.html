Fragrance campaign / NONFICTION

NONFICTION

A product story for NONFICTION centered on Gentle Night Eau de Parfum. The bottle is set against dark, tactile surfaces and coffee beans, with warm highlights tracing the glass and pale label; the film, stills, and short motion pieces each hold to that intimate visual atmosphere.

The fragrance in focus

Make Gentle Night’s bottle the point of entry.

The composition uses the bottle’s clear glass and pale label as a counterpoint to a deep, textured setting. Coffee beans bring a recognizable material into the frame, while the product name remains visible as the viewer moves from the full arrangement to closer views.

Warmth against shadow

Build atmosphere without losing label clarity.

Dark negative space gives the reflective bottle room to catch light. Controlled amber highlights reveal its edges and the texture beneath it, creating depth while keeping the branding readable and the mood consistent across differently framed photographs.

Supporting motion

Small shifts in light and texture.

Film, stills, and short studies

A compact campaign with more than one pace.

The lead film introduces the product world; two stills hold the bottle and its setting in carefully composed frames. Supporting motion studies isolate smaller changes in light and surface, extending the same visual idea into short, focused moments.
