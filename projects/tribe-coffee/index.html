Brand project / case study

TRIBE Coffee

A product story built around three coffee blends and the mythology in their names.

Project focus
Product photography
Deliverables
Brand film + stills
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

Project focus

What the story needed to show.

The three blends—Guiding Light, Creature From The Deep, and The Dread Pirate Roberts—each bring a nautical reference into the product story.

Creative approach

Keep the product and its point of view clear.

Dark walnut, candlelight, glass carafes, and a gold measuring spoon connect the coffee ritual to those references. Each blend has its own mood while remaining part of the same TRIBE identity.

Final set

A finished story across motion and stills.

The selected film leads a fifteen-image sequence spanning packaging, prepared coffee, tools, and overhead compositions.
