Coffee / three blends / product story

TRIBE Coffee

A product story for TRIBE Coffee built around three blends whose names suggest their own worlds: Guiding Light, Creature From The Deep, and The Dread Pirate Roberts. The film and fifteen-image sequence connect those references to the familiar acts of measuring, brewing, and serving coffee, while keeping the three products within one TRIBE identity.

Three blends, three cues

Let the names guide the mood of the product images.

Guiding Light, Creature From The Deep, and The Dread Pirate Roberts each introduce a different nautical reference. The photographs give those names room to shape the atmosphere around each blend, then bring the products back together as a recognizable family.

The coffee ritual

Build the story from tools, ingredients, and a finished cup.

Dark walnut, candlelight, glass carafes, and a gold measuring spoon create a tactile setting for the products. Details of preparation and serving make the coffee feel present and usable, while overhead and closer compositions add variety to the visual rhythm.

A fifteen-image sequence

Move from package to ritual and back again.

The lead film introduces the tone; the still sequence expands it across packaging, prepared coffee, tools, and overhead views. The images provide multiple entry points for the three blends while maintaining a coherent atmosphere across the complete collection.
