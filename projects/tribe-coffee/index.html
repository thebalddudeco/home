TRIBE Coffee

A product story built around three coffee blends and the mythology in their names.

Project
TRIBE Coffee
Focus
Product photography
Creative
Creative direction

The project

Give each blend its own atmosphere.

Three blends gave the project three different visual starting points: a lighthouse, a kraken, and a tall ship. The direction brought those stories into the product images through dark walnut, candlelight, glass carafes, and a gold measuring spoon. The aim was to make the coffee craft and the mythology feel part of the same world.

The direction

Three names. Three distinct moods.

Guiding Light, Creature From The Deep, and The Dread Pirate Roberts each needed a visual cue of their own while still belonging to the same TRIBE identity. I used the blends’ nautical references as a starting point, then kept the coffee and its ritual at the center of every image.
