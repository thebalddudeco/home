Brand project / case study

Forchewn

A specialty instant coffee product story told through its packaging, blends, and the ritual of a cup.

Project focus
Specialty coffee
Deliverables
Brand film + product stills
Photographer
Justin Von Braun

Project focus

What the story needed to show.

The product line brings three coffee blends together under one identity. The photographs connect the packages with the ingredients and small rituals around preparing a cup.

Creative approach

Keep the product and its point of view clear.

Warm autumn light, wood, apples, wheat, and a prepared cup create a tactile setting. The stills alternate between a full collection view and individual blend details so the packaging remains clear.

Final set

A finished story across motion and stills.

The final set includes the selected film and a sequence of product photographs designed to hold together as one visual story while letting each blend read on its own.
