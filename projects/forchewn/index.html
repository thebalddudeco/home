The project

Make the product feel like a ritual.

Forchewn’s three roasts each carry a different character: Gesha-1420 calls for clean space and precision, Citrus Rica sits comfortably in deeper tones, and Melon Vision brings a warmer palette. The visual direction ties the packaging to the rituals around a cup, balancing considered product photographs with the warmth and texture of the setting.

The direction

Let the coffee set the pace.

The product photographs were made in autumn light in the Catskills, using restrained packaging compositions alongside wheat, apples, cinnamon, and a prepared cup. That warm, tactile setting gave the three roast profiles a shared visual language while leaving room for each one to feel distinct.
