Sigma × Spring Street

A documentary look at Sigma’s Spring Street pop-up, bringing the products, space, and people into one visual story.

Project
Sigma × Spring Street
Format
Brand activation photography
Motion
Documentary film

The project

Document the experience, not just the room.

This project brings together event photography and a short documentary film. The gallery follows the activation through its product displays, visitor interactions, and quieter in-between moments.

The approach

Give the activation a life beyond the day.

The still photographs hold onto the people, product details, and atmosphere of the Spring Street pop-up. A documentary film adds movement and sequence, so the project can work as both a record of the event and a piece of brand storytelling after it closes.
