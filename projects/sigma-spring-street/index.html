Sigma × Spring Street

A documentary record of Sigma’s Spring Street pop-up told through its camera displays, the people exploring them, and the atmosphere of the space. The work combines a short documentary film with an extensive still gallery, recording the activation as an event people experienced rather than simply as a room prepared for display.

Inside the pop-up

Photograph the encounter between visitors and the products.

The gallery follows people as they move through the activation and spend time with the displayed equipment. Product details establish what is on view; portraits and interactions show how visitors respond and make the space feel active.

A record with a life beyond the event

Preserve sequence, detail, and atmosphere.

The 69 photographs range across displays, visitor moments, and quieter details that help reconstruct the environment. The documentary film adds a sense of duration and movement, giving the project a second way to communicate the experience after the pop-up has ended.
