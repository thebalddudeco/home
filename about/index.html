JUSTIN VON BRAUN / BROOKLYN, NEW YORK

I photograph brands at work and their products up close.

I’m Justin, the photographer and filmmaker behind The Bald Dude Co. I create photography and short films for brand activations, products, and campaigns in New York City and worldwide.

Some projects call for being in the room, catching people discovering a brand. Others call for a closer look at the product itself. I work across both, shaping imagery around what makes each brand distinct.
